# 🚀 Databricks 14 Days AI Challenge – Complete Journey  
**Organized by:** Indian Data Club & Codebasics  
**Sponsored by:** Databricks  
📅 **Duration:** 9 Jan 2026 – 22 Jan 2026  

---

## 📌 Overview

This repository documents my complete learning journey through the **Databricks 14 Days AI Challenge**, where I built strong foundations in **Data Engineering, Analytics, Machine Learning, MLOps, and Generative AI** using the Databricks Lakehouse platform.

The challenge followed a **hands-on, industry-aligned roadmap**, covering everything from Apache Spark fundamentals to AI-powered analytics with Genie and Mosaic AI.

---

## 🧠 What I Learned

- Apache Spark & PySpark fundamentals  
- Delta Lake & Lakehouse Architecture  
- Medallion Architecture (Bronze → Silver → Gold)  
- Data governance with Unity Catalog  
- SQL Analytics & Dashboarding  
- Performance optimization techniques  
- Statistical analysis & ML preparation  
- MLOps using MLflow  
- Model comparison & Spark ML Pipelines  
- AI-powered analytics using Genie & Mosaic AI
