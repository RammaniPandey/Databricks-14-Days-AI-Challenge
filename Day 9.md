## 📊 Day 9 (17/01/26) – SQL Analytics & Dashboards  
**Databricks 14 Days AI Challenge**

---

## 📌 Overview

Day 9 focused on **SQL Analytics and Dashboarding** using **Databricks SQL**.  
The goal was to transform curated Lakehouse data into **business insights** through analytical queries, visualizations, and interactive dashboards.

This day bridges **data engineering outputs** with **business decision-making**.

---

## 📘 Learnings

- SQL Warehouses and analytics compute
- Writing complex analytical SQL queries
- Dashboard creation in Databricks SQL
- Visualizations, filters, and scheduled refresh

---

## 🛠️ Tasks Completed

1. Created a **SQL Warehouse**
2. Wrote **analytical SQL queries**
3. Built dashboards for:
   - Revenue trends
   - Conversion funnels
   - Top-performing products
4. Added **filters** and **scheduled refresh**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

- Identified **revenue trends** using moving averages  
- Analyzed **conversion rates** across product categories  
- Segmented customers into **VIP, Loyal, and Regular** tiers  
- Enabled **interactive filtering** for business users  

---

## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Databricks SQL enables **fast analytics directly on the Lakehouse**  
- Window functions are powerful for **trend and time-series analysis**  
- Dashboards convert raw data into **decision-ready insights**  
- Filters and scheduling make dashboards **production-ready**  
- SQL analytics bridges the gap between **data engineering and BI**
