# 📅 Day 13 – Model Comparison & Feature Engineering  
**Databricks 14 Days AI Challenge**

---

## 📌 Overview

Day 13 focused on **model comparison, feature engineering, and scalable ML workflows**.  
The objective was to train multiple models, compare their performance using MLflow, and build a **Spark ML Pipeline** to support production-scale machine learning.

This day represents a key step in moving from experimentation to **model selection and standardization**.

---

## 📘 Learnings

- Training and evaluating **multiple ML models**
- Understanding **hyperparameter tuning**
- Interpreting **feature importance**
- Building scalable **Spark ML Pipelines**
- Comparing experiments using **MLflow**

---

## 🛠️ Tasks Completed

1. Trained **three different models**
2. Logged and compared **metrics in MLflow**
3. Built a **Spark ML Pipeline**
4. Selected the **best-performing model**

---

        
## 💡 Key Takeaways

- Comparing multiple models helps identify **trade-offs between simplicity and performance**
- Feature engineering plays a **major role in model accuracy**
- MLflow enables **transparent experiment comparison**
- Spark ML Pipelines ensure **scalability and reusability**
- Model selection should be **metric-driven, not assumption-driven**
